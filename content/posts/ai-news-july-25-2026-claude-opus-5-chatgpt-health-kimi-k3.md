Anthropic Officially Launches Claude Opus 5
Anthropic has officially released Claude Opus 5, positioning it as a cost-efficient powerhouse that delivers nearly all the intelligence of its flagship Claude Fable 5 at roughly half the cost.
Key Highlights:
Pricing: $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens — unchanged from Opus 4.8
Default Model: Now the default on Claude Max, Anthropic’s premium consumer tier
Performance: Early customers report 26% fewer tokens on average (Harvey) and 9 percentage points higher accuracy on financial modeling (Fundamental Research Lab)
Safety: Safety classifiers engage ~85% less often than Fable 5, with lighter-touch safeguards
Scientific Use: Anthropic’s most capable generally available model for biology-adjacent research
"Opus 5 matched Opus 4.8's maximum-reasoning performance while generating 26 percent fewer tokens on average." — Niko Grupen, Head of Applied Research, Harvey
The launch comes as Anthropic prepares for its IPO, having confidentially filed an S-1 with the SEC on June 1 following a $65 billion funding round that valued the company at roughly $965 billion.
Why it matters: With five distinct price-performance tiers (Sonnet 5, Fable 5, Opus 5, Mythos 5), Anthropic is signaling to public-market investors that its unit economics are improving, not just its benchmark scores.
OpenAI Makes ChatGPT Health Available to All US Adults
OpenAI is rolling out ChatGPT Health to all logged-in US users aged 18 and older across every plan — Free, Go, Plus, and Pro. The nationwide expansion comes just one day after a Florida-based pastor sued OpenAI, alleging ChatGPT gave his family member a near-fatal suggestion not to consult a doctor.
What ChatGPT Health Offers:
Secure connection to Apple Health, Function, MyFitnessPal, and personal health records
A unified dashboard covering lab results, medications, activity, and sleep
Personalized health Q&A grounded in your actual medical data
Permission-based access: ChatGPT asks before using connected records
Data deletion within 30 days if a health source is disconnected
Users were already sending roughly 300 million health-related queries per week inside ChatGPT before this dedicated feature existed.
The tension: Expanding a health-focused AI feature while facing a lawsuit over medical advice puts OpenAI in a precarious position. The company’s bet is that better-grounded, records-connected answers reduce risk — but regulators and plaintiffs will now test that claim in public.
Independent Testing Finds Kimi K3’s Hallucination Rate at 51%
Moonshot AI’s Kimi K3 — a 2.8 trillion-parameter open-weight model and the largest ever released — is set to publish its full weights on July 27, 2026. But independent testing from Artificial Analysis has raised eyebrows.
The Numbers:
Table
Metric	Kimi K2.6	Kimi K3
Hallucination Rate	39%	51%
Factual Accuracy	33%	46%
Despite improved factual accuracy, K3 invents wrong answers more often than its predecessor
For context, Claude Fable 5 posts a 54.9% hallucination rate on the same benchmark
K3 topped Arena.ai’s Frontend Code leaderboard, beating Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol
The open-weight release (under a Modified MIT license) will let outside researchers independently verify these figures — a critical moment for transparency in AI.
Why it matters: Enterprises now face a genuine trade-off: a near-frontier coding model at roughly one-third the price of Claude Fable 5, but with a documented honesty regression that Moonshot itself did not publish.
AI Chip Startup Etched Raises $300M at $10.3B Valuation
Etched, a Cupertino-based startup building transformer-specific AI chips (no GPUs involved), closed a $300 million Series C at a $10.3 billion valuation — doubling its worth from just seven months ago.
Investment Details:
Lead Investor: Sequoia Capital
Participants: Andreessen Horowitz, SK Hynix, Jane Street, Diffusion Capital
Early Backers: Peter Thiel, Andrej Karpathy, Dylan Field, Amjad Masad
What Etched Builds:
Sohu chips: Transformer-specific silicon on TSMC’s 4nm process
Claim: ~20x inference speedup over Nvidia H100 for transformer workloads
Orders: Already booked $1 billion in customer orders
Facility: Recently opened an 80,000 sq. ft. production facility near San Jose
The thesis: As inference (running trained models) consumes a growing majority of AI compute, general-purpose GPUs waste silicon on capabilities inference doesn’t need. Etched — alongside rivals like Cerebras, Groq, and SambaNova — is racing to fill that gap.
Genesis AI in Talks to Raise $500 Million for Humanoid Robotics
Genesis AI, a humanoid robotics startup, is reportedly in talks to raise approximately $500 million at a $3 billion pre-money valuation.
This reflects a broader investor shift toward physical AI — applying large-model reasoning to robots and embodied systems rather than chat interfaces. The theory: foundation models trained on text and code are approaching diminishing returns on language tasks, while robotics remains underexplored territory.
The challenge: Language models never had to solve getting a physical body to do useful, repeatable work outside a lab.
EU AI Act: Biggest Enforcement Deadline Just Over a Week Away
August 2, 2026 marks the most consequential milestone of the EU AI Act — when high-risk system obligations and transparency rules become binding across the bloc.
What You Need to Know:
Extraterritorial reach: Applies to any company placing an AI system on the EU market, regardless of headquarters
Scope: Most deployed AI systems must comply with transparency and risk-management rules
Consequence: Firms with EU exposure that treated compliance as a "someday problem" now have single-digit days to close the gap
The European Commission has published guidelines on transparency obligations and unveiled a coordinated EU Action Plan on Cybersecurity and AI to give companies clearer footing before the deadline.
This is the first time any jurisdiction has imposed broad, binding regulation specifically on artificial intelligence.
